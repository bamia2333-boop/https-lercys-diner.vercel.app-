Good to know

Frequently asked

Do you have a buffet?

Yes — a cafeteria-style steam-table line. Pick your entrees and sides as you walk down the line, then pay at the end.

What’s included in the lunch special?

One entree, two starches or vegetables, a bread of choice, and a dessert. That’s $14.99. A drink is $2.99.

Are you open for dinner?

No. We’re a lunch-only diner, open 10:30 AM – 2:00 PM.

Are you open on Saturdays?

No, we’re closed Saturdays. We’re open Monday through Friday and Sunday.

Do you take cards?

Yes, we take cards — but cash is preferred and always appreciated.

Is there parking?

Yes. There’s a free parking lot and free street parking.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through Uber Eats, DoorDash, Seamless and Postmates. Curbside pickup and takeout are also available.

Is it kid-friendly?

Very. There’s a kids menu for ages 10 and under, plus a kids’ buffet.

Where exactly are you?

513 6th St N in downtown Texas City, across from the Showboat.

Do you take reservations?

We’re walk-in only. Just come on in during lunch.
