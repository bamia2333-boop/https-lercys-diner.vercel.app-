Why people love it

Generous portions

Under $20 and you can’t beat the amount of food you get. Reviewers call the portions “huge.”

Comfort food done right

Chicken fried steak, fried catfish and cheeseburgers come up again and again. One reviewer called it “Grandma’s Southern kitchen.”

Friendly, clean, welcoming

Energetic staff, a clean dining room, comfortable atmosphere, and convenient free parking.
