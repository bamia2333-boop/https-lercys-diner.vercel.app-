Our story

Joy, Mark, and a leap of faith

Joy worked in the medical field. Mark spent twenty years in sales. Then they decided it was time for something new — so they opened a restaurant in their hometown of Winnie, Texas.

“This is going to be our new home.” Joy Cooper, co-owner — opening day, May 27, 2025

Mark runs the front of house. Joy runs the kitchen. Between them they built Lercy’s from one location in Winnie, to a second in Mont Belvieu, and now a third here in Texas City.

“It’s always about people.”
