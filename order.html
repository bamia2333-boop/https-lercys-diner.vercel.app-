Three ways to get your food

Pickup, curbside, or delivery

Counter pickup

Call ahead, then walk in and grab it. Free parking lot and free street parking right outside.

Curbside

Pull up out front on 6th Street, call us, and we’ll bring your order out to your car. Across from the Showboat.

Delivery

Use Uber Eats, DoorDash, Seamless or Postmates. Delivery runs during our lunch window, 10:30 AM to 2:00 PM.

Heads up: we’re lunch only. Once we close at 2:00 PM, we’re done for the day. And when a dish sells out on the line, it’s gone — call ahead if you’re coming for something specific.
